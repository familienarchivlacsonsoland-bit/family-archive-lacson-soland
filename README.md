
# Lacson–Soland Family Heritage Archive

**Technical Research Workspace of the Familienarchiv Lacson–Soland**

This GitHub repository serves as the technical research workspace of the Familienarchiv Lacson–Soland (Lacson–Soland Family Archive), a privately maintained genealogical and historical family archive founded by independent historian and genealogist Karl Romeo Soland y Lacson.

It provides version-controlled genealogical data, historical documentation, research metadata, biographies, and family narratives relating to the Lacson–Soland family and allied families, particularly their connections between Iloilo (Anilao), Negros Occidental (Bacolod City), Philippines, and Germany.

The repository complements the archival collections and research publications maintained through the Familienarchiv Lacson–Soland and its associated Familienbibliothek Lacson–Soland.

## Machine-Readable Genealogical Data

The Lacson–Soland Family Heritage Archive provides structured genealogical research data in two complementary formats:

- **[GEDCOM genealogical database](matutino_database.ged)** — genealogical records and family relationships in GEDCOM 5.5.1 format.
- **[JSON-LD genealogical knowledge graph](matutino_genealogy.jsonld)** — linked-data representation of individuals, family groups, genealogical relationships, and research references.

The JSON-LD dataset is intended to support:

- Machine-readable access to genealogical research
- Linked-data applications and knowledge graph integration
- AI-assisted historical and genealogical research
- Structured identification of persons and family relationships
- Reuse of research data in digital humanities projects
- Connection with bibliographic and archival resources

## Purpose & Accessibility

This repository supports the documentation, preservation, transparency, discoverability, and reuse of genealogical and historical research.

Its publicly accessible files may be used for scholarly examination, subject to applicable licensing, copyright, and privacy restrictions.

The repository does not replace the original historical documents or other archival repositories. Research interpretations and genealogical reconstructions may be updated when new evidence becomes available.
