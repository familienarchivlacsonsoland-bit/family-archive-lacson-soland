# Lacson–Soland Family Heritage Archive

**Technical Research Workspace of the Familienarchiv Lacson–Soland**

This GitHub repository serves as the technical research workspace of the **Familienarchiv Lacson–Soland (Lacson–Soland Family Archive)**, a privately maintained genealogical and historical family archive founded by independent historian and genealogist Karl Romeo Soland y Lacson.

It provides version-controlled genealogical data, historical documentation, research metadata, biographies, and family narratives relating to the **Lacson–Soland family and allied families**, particularly their connections between Iloilo (Anilao), Negros Occidental (Bacolod City), Philippines, and Germany.

The repository complements the archival collections and research publications maintained through the Familienarchiv Lacson–Soland and its associated Familienbibliothek Lacson–Soland.

## Research Sources & Documentation

The genealogical documentation in this repository is associated with historical research, archival references, and structured family records. These materials provide a basis for examining family relationships and historical interpretations.

### Research Files

**`matutino-thesenpapier_metadata.json`**

Metadata associated with the research publication *Die Matutinos von Anilao: Eine Analyse indigener Nobilität auf Panay*, including bibliographic and descriptive information.

**`matutino_database.ged`**

GEDCOM genealogical database containing structured family data relating to the Matutino lineage of Anilao (Iloilo, Panay), including Nicolasa Arada, Don Guillermo Arada, Doña Leoncia Matutino, and connected family members.

**[ahnenliste_nicolasa_arada.md](ahnenliste_nicolasa_arada.md)**

Genealogical documentation relating to Nicolasa Arada.
